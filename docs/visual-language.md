# Visual language

Problem Field should feel calm, warm, and spacious without turning its field metaphor into a theme. The atmosphere belongs at the edges: color temperature, type, light, and the small horizon mark. The work itself stays clear, quiet, and evidence-led.

## Principles

1. **Calm is functional.** Keep the canvas central and move controls between the periphery and the foreground only when they are needed.
2. **Expression clarifies hierarchy.** Warm color, rounded geometry, and depth should identify the functional layer or an important action—not decorate every surface.
3. **One obvious next step.** Empty states and sheets should have one primary action. Secondary actions appear only when context makes them useful.
4. **Tactile, not skeuomorphic.** Use warm paper surfaces, hairline borders, and low, broad shadows. Avoid literal paper textures, glass everywhere, or ornamental scenery.
5. **Pastoral, not cute.** The field idea appears in the logo, palette, and spatial openness. Avoid puns, botanical decoration, emoji, folksy labels, and whimsical system copy.

## System

- **Display type:** Newsreader, designed for expressive, sustained on-screen reading.
- **Interface type:** Manrope, a restrained geometric sans with enough warmth to avoid a generic system feel.
- **Core palette:** warm ivory canvas, deep moss actions, quiet sage support, and a single golden-hour accent.
- **Surfaces:** opaque enough for legibility, translucent only in the floating functional layer.
- **Iconography:** Lucide throughout the interface; the custom mark is reserved for identity.
- **Motion:** brief state transitions only, with reduced-motion support and no ambient animation.

## Information architecture guardrails

- Keep the floating dock as the single persistent control surface.
- Hide review controls until there is agent work to review.
- Do not repeat the suggested next move over an empty-state call to action.
- Reveal source, loop, add, and review detail in one sheet at a time.
- Keep agent interpretation provisional and visually separate from human-accepted field content.

## Research basis

- [Calm Technology principles](https://calmtech.com/) — require the smallest useful amount of attention and give people what they need to solve the problem, no more.
- [Google's Material 3 Expressive research](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research?pubDate=20250521) — expression works when color, shape, size, motion, and containment guide attention and create a deliberate emotional response.
- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines: Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) — reserve floating material for functional layers and use it sparingly so content remains primary.
- [Production Type: Newsreader](https://productiontype.com/font/newsreader) — a contemporary, bookish family designed for on-screen reading without losing personality.
- [Creative Bloq: 2026 graphic design trends](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/graphic-design/texture-warmth-and-tactile-rebellion-the-big-graphic-design-trends-for-2026) — current visual culture is moving toward warmer, tactile, distinctly human work in response to synthetic polish.

These are inputs, not a trend checklist. Product clarity and evidence legibility outrank stylistic novelty.
