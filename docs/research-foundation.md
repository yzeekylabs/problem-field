# Research foundation — July 2026

The product should not expose a pile of named frameworks. It should turn the strongest compatible parts into one teachable practice.

## Frameworks selected

### 1. Sensemaking Loop — the macro interaction model

Pirolli and Card distinguish **information foraging** (find, filter, extract, organize) from **sensemaking** (form and test frames), with iteration between them. Current spatial-synthesis research reinforces keeping sources and emerging structure close together rather than splitting them across tools.

- [Sensemaking chapter, 2026](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-09307-3_2)
- [Human-in-the-loop spatial synthesis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214579618302995)

Product consequence: the canvas is the continuous surface; sources, clusters, frames, and questions are modes of the same recursive loop, not separate workflows.

### 2. Reflexive Thematic Analysis — how interpretation develops

Braun and Clarke describe six recursive phases: familiarisation, coding, generating themes, developing/reviewing themes, refining/naming themes, and writing up. They explicitly reject a rigid linear recipe and treat researcher interpretation as situated rather than mechanically objective.

- [Braun & Clarke: doing reflexive thematic analysis](https://www.thematicanalysis.net/doing-reflexive-ta/)

Product consequence: AI may suggest clusters and frames, but a human names, splits, merges, challenges, and owns them.

### 3. Atomic Research — the traceability model

The Experiments → Facts → Insights → Recommendations model keeps findings reusable and many-to-many rather than burying them in reports.

- [Tomer Sharon on Atomic Research](https://tsharon.medium.com/the-three-most-popular-questions-about-atomic-research-56a5414d3d17)
- [2026 comparison of Atomic Research, thematic analysis, and affinity mapping](https://dovetail.com/research/atomic-research-vs-thematic-analysis-vs-affinity-mapping/)

Product consequence: source, evidence, observation, pattern, and decision remain distinct typed objects with inspectable relationships.

### 4. Analysis of Competing Hypotheses — the anti-confirmation-bias move

ACH makes alternatives explicit and asks which evidence is inconsistent with each explanation, rather than only collecting confirming examples.

- [CIA Tradecraft Primer](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/955180a45afe3f5013772c313b16face/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)

Product consequence: “Test” actively seeks contrary evidence, segment differences, and plausible alternative frames.

### 5. Information power and meaning saturation — signal calibration

Hennink and colleagues distinguish hearing the range of issues (code saturation) from understanding their depth and nuance (meaning saturation). Malterud’s information-power model argues that adequacy depends on the aim, sample specificity, theory, dialogue quality, and analysis—not a universal interview count.

- [Code saturation versus meaning saturation](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1049732316665344)
- [Information power](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26613970/)
- [2026 review of qualitative saturation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1048984326000093)

Product consequence: no fake confidence percentage. Pattern signal exposes recurrence, independent source breadth, contradiction, and eventually consequence/workaround, segment concentration, and freshness.

### 6. Decision-first continuous discovery — why the field exists

Decision-first research begins with what will change because of the learning. Opportunity Solution Trees connect outcomes to opportunities, solutions, and assumption tests, while distinguishing generative interviews from evaluative testing.

- [Behzod Sirjani: decision-first user research](https://www.reforge.com/blog/your-user-research-questions-answered-a-recap-of-our-ama-with-behzod-sirjani)
- [Teresa Torres: discovering solutions](https://www.producttalk.org/discovering-solutions/)

Product consequence: every field has one active focus and ends by making the next inquiry explicit. This product deliberately stops before solution selection unless evidence justifies moving there.

## AI posture

Current work supports a hybrid workflow: LLMs can help code, group, retrieve quotes, and visualize material, but subjective labeling shifts and methodological flattening remain real risks.

- [Hybrid human–AI reflexive thematic analysis](https://repository.isls.org/handle/1/11227)
- [GATOS: a 2026 open-source GenAI codebook workflow](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-026-06508-5)
- [2026 study of LLM-assisted thematic analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-026-01088-4)
- [2025 evidence that LLM assistance can alter subjective label distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15821)
- [Microsoft HAX guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/)
- [Google PAIR: explainability and trust](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapter/explainability-trust/)
- [Microsoft human-centered agent design, updated 2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/agents/design-guidelines/human-centered-design)

The operating rule is: suggestions are attributable, evidence-linked, scoped, correctable, dismissible, and provisional. AI never silently becomes canonical understanding.

## 2026 market read

Current products increasingly converge on source-linked themes, AI query, OCR/transcription, and spatial or repository synthesis:

- [Fabric](https://fabric.so/for/user-research) — spatial evidence and AI query.
- [Dovetail](https://dovetail.com/solutions/research-repository/) — repository memory and source-traceable themes.
- [Indeemo](https://indeemo.com/platform/analyse) — multimodal extraction and source-linked patterns.
- [Great Question](https://greatquestion.co/support/repository/synthesis) — citation-backed AI synthesis plus manual groups.
- [Verithos](https://verithos.co/) — finding → code → quote → source chains.

The defensible wedge is therefore not “AI finds themes.” It is a clean, spatial, novice-guided practice for judging what deserves focus—while keeping evidence, uncertainty, and the next decision one move away.
